<p>Biometric Bypass is an Xposed module that makes face unlock feel instant by skipping the confirmation step Android enforces after biometric authentication.</p>
<p>Even after your face is recognized, Android often shows a "tap to confirm" dialog before letting you through. This module removes that dialog system-wide, so a successful scan unlocks straight away instead of waiting for a second tap.</p>
<p>It works by hooking into System UI, so the bypass applies everywhere, including banking and security-sensitive apps that would normally force the extra step.</p>
<p><b>Requirements</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Android 10 (API 29) or higher</li>
<li>An Xposed framework with libxposed API 101 support</li>
<li>An AOSP-based or close-to-stock ROM (OEM skins like MIUI or One UI are untested and likely unsupported)</li>
</ul>
<p><b>A note on security</b></p>
<p>Removing the confirmation step trades a little security for speed. With it enabled, anything that satisfies the biometric sensor, including a spoof or your own face while you are asleep, will unlock without the extra tap. Use it where that tradeoff makes sense for you.</p>
