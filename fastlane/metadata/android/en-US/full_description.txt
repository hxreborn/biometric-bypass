<p>Xposed module that skips the "tap to confirm" dialog Android shows after face unlock. It taps confirm for you in System UI, so it works in all apps.</p>
<p><b>Requirements</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Android 10 (API 29) or newer</li>
<li>Xposed framework with libxposed API 101 (recent LSPosed)</li>
<li>AOSP or close-to-stock ROM. OEM skins like MIUI or One UI may not work.</li>
</ul>
